# Brag Plan: Lerna AI

## What is this app?
Lerna AI is a study workspace where students upload course materials, ask an AI tutor questions, keep study sessions, and make practice quizzes.

## The angle
Turn a dense biology handout into an understandable explanation and a quick self-check. The video should feel like studying becoming lighter, while showing Lerna's actual upload, tutor, and quiz interfaces.

## Hook (first 2.5 seconds)
On a deep charcoal canvas, the line "Your notes have questions." appears beside a compact stack of lecture pages. The stack resolves into Lerna's cyan-accented workspace.

## Key moments
- A fictional `Cell Division Notes.pdf` enters the real "Drop files here" upload workspace and becomes a searchable session file.
- In the tutor, a student asks "Explain mitosis simply." A concise answer builds in the chat area.
- A practice question appears in the quiz interface: "What separates during anaphase?" The answer "Sister chromatids" is selected.

## Outro / punchline
Lerna AI. "Learn smarter, not harder." End on a clear brand mark and a settled product view.

## User flow worth showing
Upload study material → ask the tutor about it → test understanding with a quiz. The file name, chat, and quiz content are fictional demonstrations, with no customer or student data.

## Planning rubric
1. App: an AI-powered workspace for study materials, tutoring, sessions, and practice quizzes.
2. Strongest claim: "Learn smarter, not harder."
3. Visual hook: the charcoal interface with a cyan highlight and a study-file card.
4. Actual UI: upload drop zone, tutor chat, and quiz question card.
5. Shortest satisfying video: 20 seconds.
6. Tone: app-store; creative direction: focused, calm academic confidence.
7. Audio: clean, low music bed with a few restrained UI taps and card arrivals.
8. Share caption: "Turn notes into questions you can answer. Lerna AI brings your materials, tutor, and practice into one study session."
9. User flow: upload → ask → quiz.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: focused, calm academic confidence
- Interpretation: clean type, controlled slides, deliberate UI interactions, and room to read each line.

## Format: landscape — 1280x720
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#0a0a0a` (landing page); dashboard charcoal is `hsl(220 6% 6%)`.
- Accent: cyan `#06b6d4`, with lighter cyan and teal highlights.
- Text: white `#ffffff` and muted slate.
- Display font: Space Grotesk.
- Body font: Manrope.
- Strongest visual element: a dark study workspace with softly bordered cards and cyan action accents.

## Share copy (draft)
Turn notes into questions you can answer. Lerna AI brings your materials, tutor, and practice into one study session.

## Audio direction
- Role: understated rhythmic bed.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`.
- Music treatment: start immediately at low volume, stay beneath UI moments, and fade under the final brand hold.
- Music cue guidance: bundled vol-12 preset, about 110 BPM. Consider the 8.74-second strong cue for the tutor reveal and the 17.47-second strong cue for the final transition. Use every other beat for readable quiz text.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle cyan glow on an existing product card; no waveform graphics.
- SFX posture: sparse, soft selection and card placement cues timed to visible actions.
- Audio-coupled moments: document drop, prompt send, answer reveal, quiz selection.
- Restraint rule: no loud impacts, repeated clicks, or effects over text.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — From notes to understanding — 2.5s
Dark background. A small stack of biology lecture pages slides into view. "Your notes have questions." settles for at least 1.3 seconds. Cyan light draws a line toward the product window.
Sequential/interaction: the page stack gathers into one file card.
Audio intent: immediate, warm forward motion.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft paper/card movement.
Music: low steady bed.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Upload — 4.5s
Recreate Lerna's "Teach Lerna from your study files" workspace with its "Drop files here" area. A fictional `Cell Division Notes.pdf` is dropped; its card appears with a ready/searchable status. Keep the interface and file label readable.
Sequential/interaction: cursor clicks "Select files"; the file card appears and its status changes after arrival.
Audio intent: quiet confirmation.
Audio-coupled idea: drop and a soft ready cue.
Music: continuous.
Transition mood: smooth wipe → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Ask the tutor — 5.5s
Show Lerna's dark chat workspace. In the input, "Explain mitosis simply." is sent. The tutor reply arrives: "Mitosis is how one cell makes two matching cells." Highlight the key phrase while the rest of the UI stays still.
Sequential/interaction: visible send click, followed by answer appearing in two readable lines.
Audio intent: the music gently opens up.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle click and restrained answer-card arrival near the 8.74s cue.
Music: continuous.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Check understanding — 4s
Show a real-looking Lerna quiz card: "What separates during anaphase?" The "Sister chromatids" option is selected and a small correct state appears. Keep the question on screen long enough to read.
Sequential/interaction: cursor selects one answer; correct state follows.
Audio intent: a single positive accent.
Audio-coupled idea: tap on selection, light confirmation.
Music: continuous.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Brand hold — 3.5s
Lerna AI logo/wordmark, then the site's exact line "Learn smarter, not harder." Hold the completed composition and cyan product window at rest.
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: finish warm and calm; fade the bed.
Audio-coupled idea: final logo landing around the 17.47s strong cue if readability permits.
Music: fade out.
Transition mood: end.

**Music mood for this video:** polished and steady.
**Audio summary:** A quiet rhythmic bed and four motion-matched accents support the upload, tutor, quiz, and logo without competing with the copy.
